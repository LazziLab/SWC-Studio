# Code of Conduct

SWC-Studio follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every
background and experience level, and do not harass anyone, in issues, pull
requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in the project concerns you, email the maintainer at
**shixinra@usc.edu**. Reports are kept confidential. You will get a reply,
and the maintainer will act as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe, from a private warning up to a ban from the project.

Please do not report conduct problems in public issues.
